AI Integration at XJTLU

AI Tone A/B Test

AI Used in This Study

Career Buddy Xiaoshang is the AI used in this study.

It is an XJTLU AI agent that supports students with career-related questions and resources.

Experiment Goal

To examine whether different AI tones affect XJTLU students’ usage time, task completion, and satisfaction.

Participants

A small-sample A/B test with 20–40 XJTLU students.

Independent Variable: AI Tone

Group A: Empathetic AI

Participants use an AI with an empathetic tone.

Group B: Analytical AI

Participants use an AI with an analytical tone.

Study Design

This is a randomised experiment. Participants are randomly assigned equally to Group A or Group B, and each participant experiences only one AI tone.

GroupAI Tone
Group AEmpathetic
Group BAnalytical

Dependent Variables and Data Collection

MeasureData Collection
Average usage timeRecord the time participants spend using the AI.
Task completion rateRecord whether participants complete the task.
User satisfactionCollect responses through a questionnaire survey.
User feelingsCollect participants’ feelings through interviews.

Experimental Procedure

  1. Recruit 20–40 XJTLU students.
  2. Randomly assign participants equally to the empathetic-AI group or analytical-AI group.
  3. Participants use the assigned AI tone and complete the task.
  4. Record usage time and task completion rate.
  5. Participants complete a satisfaction questionnaire and take part in an interview about their feelings.
